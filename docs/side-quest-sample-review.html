SPARK Side Quest Sample Review

October 16, 2026. Review only: these 20 samples have not been saved to the game, scheduled, approved, or published.

Evaluation: 20 distinct concepts across 12 categories. Each has a short title, two-sentence description, and one creative goal. The batch covers cafeteria work and schoolwide appreciation, campus details, teamwork, learning, sustainability, and kindness. Existing approved examples are excluded from new draws. Seasonal duplicates are skipped when that concept is already approved. These are curated templates, not AI output.
