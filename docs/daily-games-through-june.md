# Daily games through June 4, 2027

The previous rotation had 30 Word answers and 10 Sort boards. The expansion starts Monday September 28, 2026 and covers every weekday through June 4, 2027: 180 dates, including weekday holidays so no calendar omissions can exhaust the bank.

Cafeteria Word has 201 unique five-letter answers with category clues and hints. A fixed schedule uses 180 different answers over those dates. The accepted-guess dictionary includes every answer.

SPARK Sort has 180 distinct boards drawn from 20 curated category sets with varying four-of-six item selections. Categories recur, but the complete board contents do not repeat during the covered dates. Every board has 16 different items, four groups of four, and an Easy, Medium or Hard rating. Each five-day run includes easier and harder puzzles. The rating is shown before play. No scoring or chance-count changes.

Daily puzzle identity is deterministic and shared across schools. Weekends retain Friday's puzzle. Existing dates before September 28 use the original puzzle IDs/content, preserving in-progress and completed records. Do not change the frozen word shuffle seed, reorder the banks or alter published answers; that would change saved puzzles. Future seasons should use a new date range.

No migration is required for this game update. Point corrections and rollout policy repair are separate from this change; this work only audits those records and does not modify live awards.
